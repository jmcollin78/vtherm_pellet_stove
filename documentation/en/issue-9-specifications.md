## 1. Context and objectives

Enable Pellet Stove regulation through dry contact: stove, bistable contact, HA `switch`, `over_switch` VTherm, `pellet_regulation` algorithm. No intermediate generic thermostat is required.

Every VTherm used by Pellet Stove must have **exactly one underlying**, in dry-contact and `climate` modes. Dry contact is an explicit local option, not automatic activation based on domain or inherited from a common setting.

Underlying state following, persistence/recovery, reconciliation and pending commands apply to both modes. In `climate`, retain burner phases and protections: the shared following change is intentional, not a requirement for strictly unchanged behavior. Preserve identifiers, configuration and type-specific commands, except for adaptations explicitly specified here.

The review reports blocked Pellet Stove-controlled ignition for an `over_switch` VTherm backed by a `switch`; this inherited finding is not reaudited here. This document describes target behavior, not a feature already shipped. An actually observed external interface limitation must be reported, without fictional guarantees or additional scope restrictions.

## 2. Actors and use cases

| Actor                         | Trigger and expected outcome                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| User configuring Pellet Stove | Explicitly chooses dry contact for an eligible VTherm, reads the warning before confirmation and sets durations for the appliance; other entries remain unchanged. |
| Pellet Stove regulation       | Receives current VTherm demand, observes the underlying, applies minima and safety guards, then commands only an eligible transition.                              |
| User or external command      | Changes the usable underlying state; Pellet Stove adopts it and continues regulation under the same guards, without inversion solely due to adoption.              |
| Home Assistant and underlying | Restart, become unavailable or publish a phase; Pellet Stove pauses where required and reconciles without attributing unobserved elapsed time.                     |

## 3. Functional requirements

### Observations and time

The "underlying's actual observed state" means its usable state observed and exposed to the integration. It is authoritative for adoption/reconciliation, not a guarantee of unavailable physical measurements. In dry contact it describes the contact, without proof of flame or completed physical cooling.

| Usable observation                                           | Meaning and acknowledgement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `switch.on`                                                  | Active contact / heating requested; acknowledges ON without proof of flame or heating. At startup, already-present ON is immediately adopted as on for regulation only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `switch.off`                                                 | Inactive contact / presumed cooling; acknowledges OFF. For a new OFF cycle, initializes counters at observation; matching reconciliation resumes retained counters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `climate` burner startup                                     | Acknowledges ON without waiting for actual heating.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Burner directly heating                                      | Also acknowledges ON without requiring observed startup.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Duepi-EVO burner `Cooldown`                                  | Acknowledges OFF, then track cooling; do not wait for `Off` or claim completed cooling.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Burner directly `Off`                                        | Also acknowledges OFF without requiring prior `Cooldown` or inventing its duration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Duepi burner `Cleaning`                                      | Non-heating / inactive (Q-006); observed after an OFF command, confirms heating has ended (Q-007), acknowledges OFF and immediately ends its confirmation wait (Q-008). If this observation first establishes the OFF cycle, initializes minimum OFF and cooldown at zero at that observation, never at command submission (Q-009). A previously established OFF cycle is not reset by stable state or normal progression. Proves neither physical cooling completion nor immediate ON permission; no invented past timestamp. |
| Duepi burner `Eco idle`                                      | Non-heating / inactive (Q-006); observed after an OFF command, confirms heating has ended (Q-007), acknowledges OFF and immediately ends its confirmation wait (Q-008), with the same counter start and limits as `Cleaning` (Q-009).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Requested HVAC `heat` / `off` alone                          | Proves neither heating nor cooling completion; does not replace burner observation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `unknown`, `unavailable`, missing/unclassifiable observation | No usable state; pause regulation and counters, monitor then reconcile.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

`Cooldown` is the approved functional label. Design must explicitly map target-version representations, including local-code `Cooling down`. Service return is never observed acknowledgement or physical proof.

The non-heating / inactive classification of `Cleaning` and `Eco idle` is settled, without physically equating them with `Off`, `Cooldown` or unavailability. After an OFF command, Q-007 confirms heating has ended, Q-008 acknowledges OFF and immediately ends its wait; Q-009 initializes minimum OFF and cooldown at zero at the first observation establishing the OFF cycle, never command submission, without waiting for `Cooling down` or `Off`. An established cycle retains counters on stable state/normal progression and matching recovery. Active time, pausing, monitoring, no command repetition/ON replay and accessible error still apply. Neither ON acknowledgement, completed physical cooling, immediate ON permission, a past timestamp nor cancellation of OFF priority follows. Q-009 covers the context after an OFF command; outside that context, apply shared adoption/reconciliation to every usable state without extending this acknowledgement decision.

Retain only the three existing durations, including units, values and bounds:

- `min_on_duration_min`: minimum activation before ordinary OFF, never ignition duration.
- `min_off_duration_min`: minimum OFF time before a new ON.
- `cooldown_duration_min`: dry-contact presumed cooling duration; completion is not physical proof.

The next-ON guard is `min_off_duration_min + cooldown_duration_min` since OFF observation, in accumulated active time.

Counters pause while HA is stopped and during unavailability. Expiry grants permission, not an order. Physical `climate` phases remain separate guards; do not reduce the burner to a binary relay or reset the whole cycle on each normal phase progression.

### Recovery and reconciliation

At HA shutdown, save elapsed counters and cycle state, not just configuration. Example: 20 saved minutes against a 30-minute minimum ON leave 10 minutes on recovery, regardless of downtime, if state matches.

| Retained state and first usable observation | Handling                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Matching                                    | Resume saved/retained counters without credit for stopped/unavailable time.                                      |
| Retained ON, observed OFF                   | Adopt OFF; minimum OFF and cooldown restart at zero on observation.                                              |
| Retained OFF, observed ON                   | Adopt ON; full minimum ON from observation before ordinary OFF.                                                  |
| No usable snapshot / first installation     | Adopt first usable state and zero corresponding counters.                                                        |
| Other cycle mismatch                        | Adopt usable observed state and initialize corresponding counters at zero.                                       |
| Unavailable underlying                      | Pellet Stove `unavailable`, counters/decisions paused, indefinite monitoring then same reconciliation on return. |

These rules apply at startup, reload and runtime for both types. A stable phase or normal progression within an established cycle is not a mismatch. Design specifies `climate` phase matches to apply these rules without removing protections. Do not claim to know transition time during interruption. No default shutdown. Explicit OFF/safety retains priority when command path is usable, without claiming delivery/execution during unavailability.

Reconciliation alone shall produce neither ON/OFF, additional waiting, user intervention nor reconstruction of unknown history. It shall neither cancel explicit OFF/safety nor authorize ON without guards. After adoption, only normal regulation under current demand and protections may decide a command.

Adopt usable external actions, then continue regulation under guards. Do not issue an opposite command to restore former internal state or require manual acknowledgement solely for that action. External authorship is not always identifiable.

### Pending commands and errors

After ON/OFF, retain last observed state separately from pending command. Still-published previous state, especially through polling, does not alone imply failure or external action; it remains the observed state for adoption/reconciliation. Section 3 observations may acknowledge without an observed intermediate phase; invent neither missed phase nor elapsed duration.

For `Cleaning` as well as `Eco idle` after OFF, Q-008 requires observed acknowledgement and immediate termination of the confirmation wait, including late observation or without an observed intermediate phase. Do not wait for `Cooldown` or `Off`, resend OFF, replay rejected ON or retain pending confirmation solely because neither other phase has appeared. An earlier error remains accessible without preventing this reconciliation. Other acknowledgements remain unchanged. Ending this wait is neither physical cooling completion, cancellation of OFF priority nor ON authorization. No hypothetical command history shall prolong waiting after eligible acknowledgement or prevent adoption.

After OFF, await observation indefinitely without time-based alert; keep monitoring however late. No new ON timeout or retry mechanism is added. Pending alone with an available underlying does not mean `unavailable`.

Explicit ON/OFF error: retain observation, expose error and monitor. Error proves neither transition nor absence of hardware effect. No automatic resend, including periodic evaluations; later relevant observation can confirm without intervention. Keep error accessible without a history-only hold.

Explicit OFF/safety during pending ON immediately takes priority. Reconcile late observed ON without lifting it. Conversely, silently ignore ON during ongoing stop, without memory/replay or rejection-driven state/counter changes. Do not retain rejected ON for after stop.

## 5. Functional constraints

- Safety: explicit OFF/safety overrides minima and pending ON; a HA contact observation guarantees neither physical shutdown nor heating. Any transmission or actual-order limitation must be reported.
- Availability: pending acknowledgement with an available underlying does not make Pellet Stove unavailable; an unusable observation makes it `unavailable` and pauses regulation/counters, subject to explicit OFF/safety priority when command delivery is possible.
- Compatibility: retain existing identifiers and options without implicit activation or regression of `climate`-specific phases and commands, apart from explicitly required following/reconciliation and single-underlying restriction.
- User experience: warning before confirmation and status information that cannot be mistaken for physical measurements; normal ON rejection silent, explicit command error visible.
- Privacy, regulatory obligations and availability guarantees: no further requirement established by the consulted sources.

## 7. Out-of-scope functions

Excluded from this issue: multiple underlyings, `input_boolean`, pulse commands or presumed hardware inversion (contract limited to one bistable contact); unavailable physical stove feedback; new ignition/shutdown durations and active power/boost in dry contact (existing durations and ON/OFF contact only); changes to VTherm, Duepi-EVO, firmware or stove (outside this integration's scope). Classic TPI remains a current workaround, not a behavior specified here.

## 8. Proposed future developments

Support for multiple underlyings or other contact types would require a new functional contract and user decision; it is not an issue #9 requirement. No other future change has been approved.
