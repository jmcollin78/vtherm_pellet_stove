## Partial Q-005 decision of 2026-11-05: per-VTherm dry-contact option

**Approved decision:** offer dry-contact mode as an explicit option in each VTherm's own Pellet Stove configuration. Activation is the user's choice for that VTherm, not automatic activation through a common setting. Before confirmation, show a French/English warning: the ON/OFF contact represents a heating request without confirmation of flame, actual heating or physical cooling completion; minimum durations and cooldown are time-based protections, not proof of physical state. Preserve existing entries without automatic activation or silent conversion. This confirms D-002 and settles local scope without changing resolution rules for other shared parameters.

**Additional acceptance AC-028:** configure two VTherms and explicitly enable dry-contact mode for only one after presenting the FR/EN warning: verify that only the selected VTherm changes mode. A common setting, reload or migration must not enable this mode on another VTherm; without confirmation of the choice, preserve existing mode. Verify visibility of limitations before confirmation and no conversion of existing entries.

**Power and boost, approved on 2026-11-05:** in dry-contact mode, hide power and boost settings in the form and make them ineffective at runtime. No legacy, shared or default value may reactivate those functions or produce power/boost commands in this mode. Preserve previously stored values without deleting them or replacing them with neutralized values; they remain available for an explicit return to `climate` mode under its configuration rules. Do not change values or behavior of entries remaining in `climate` mode. This settles historical references to presentation/neutralization remaining open.

**Additional acceptance AC-029:** start with configured power/boost, explicitly enable dry contact and verify that those settings are hidden and ineffective in regulation and commands, including after saving, reload and restart. Verify preservation of legacy values despite editing the form where they are hidden; shared or default values must not bypass neutralization. Explicitly return to `climate` mode: previous values are available again under that mode's rules. An unconverted `climate` entry retains its power/boost settings and behavior.

**Single underlying, approved on 2026-11-05:** every VTherm used by the Pellet Stove integration must have exactly one underlying, in both dry-contact and `climate` modes. Do not restrict this constraint to dry contact or arbitrarily select an underlying when several are declared. Dry-contact mode requires an `over_switch` VTherm with one `switch` underlying controlled through ON/OFF; the user confirms the contact is bistable, which Home Assistant states cannot physically authenticate. Validate declared configuration without presenting this as hardware detection. Zero or multiple underlyings violate the Pellet Stove contract. Single-underlying `climate` configurations remain in scope.

**Additional acceptance AC-030:** verify exactly one underlying for both dry-contact and `climate` configurations: accept cardinality one, reject zero or multiple without silent entity selection. For dry contact, also verify `over_switch`, `switch` domain and ON/OFF commands; present user confirmation of bistable contact without claiming HA proof. Verify that validation alone does not convert a single-underlying `climate` configuration to dry contact. Design must specify validation points and handling of configurations becoming noncompliant after saving.

**Q-005: functional decisions closed on 2026-11-05.** Explicit local option and warning, power/boost hiding/neutralization with value preservation, and a single underlying for all Pellet Stove VTherms are decided. This supersedes historical open-Q-005 references and explicitly extends the single-underlying constraint to `climate` mode. Technical design remains unfinished, including validation and noncompliant configuration handling; implementation not authorized.

## Partial Q-004 decision of 2026-11-05: stop pending and Cooldown

**Approved decision:** after requesting stop, expose an intermediate “stop pending” state separate from last observed state. For a Duepi-EVO `climate`, the burner transition to **`Cooldown`** acknowledges the request and ends pending. `Off` occurs later: do not wait for it to acknowledge stop or interpret `Cooldown` as completed cooling. Continue tracking the cooling phase. HVAC remaining `heat` alone does not contradict burner observation.

Service return is not this acknowledgement. Previous heating state still published because of polling does not alone justify an immediate alert, assumed external action, repeating orders on every evaluation or an automatic opposite command. Waiting alone does not make Pellet Stove `unavailable` while the underlying remains available; actual unavailability follows Q-002. Do not fictitiously advance OFF/cooling counters from the command alone.

**Label and mapping:** `Cooldown` is the exact label specified by the user. The inspected local Duepi-EVO code exposes `Cooling down`: verify this version/representation difference and explicitly map it during design, without replacing the approved term or changing Duepi-EVO in this documentation phase.

**Additional acceptance AC-019:** request stop, repeatedly publish previous heating state, then observe burner `Cooldown`: remain pending before that observation without an immediate alert or a command induced solely by delay, then acknowledge and track cooling without claiming completion. Later `Off` must not delay acknowledgement or trigger another command. Verify availability while pending, Q-002 on actual unavailability, and mapping for the target Duepi-EVO version.

**No-expiry waiting, approved on 2026-11-05:** remain “awaiting confirmation” for as long as stop acknowledgement is not observed, even if it never arrives. No timeout, alert or failure inferred solely from elapsed time; no automatic retry or opposite command solely because of waiting. Keep monitoring the underlying and automatically reconcile when a relevant observation arrives, however late. Without confirmation, do not claim a completed stop or invent a transition or cooling progress. Absence of an event is not confirmation; reconciliation requires a usable observation. Waiting does not override explicit OFF/safety priorities or Q-002 on actual unavailability; explicitly reported errors remain a separate decision. This settles anomaly timeout without guaranteeing physical shutdown.

**Additional acceptance AC-020:** with the underlying available, leave stop unacknowledged for a long time and repeat previous state: retain pending confirmation and last observed state without time-based alerts, extra orders induced by waiting or invented cooling transitions/counters. With no subsequent confirmation, remain pending. Then deliver a late acknowledgement observation: automatically reconcile and continue tracking without user action or a reconciliation command. For Duepi-EVO, verify `Cooldown` acknowledgement according to AC-019.

**Direct Off observation, approved on 2026-11-05:** if the Duepi-EVO burner is directly observed as `Off` after the stop request, accept it as confirmation and automatically reconcile without requiring previously observed `Cooldown`. Leave confirmation pending and adopt the reported stopped phase; do not invent a past cooling phase/duration or backdate counters. This requires usable burner observation, not requested HVAC mode alone. Confirmation itself issues no command and does not waive guards on the next ON, including minimum durations and contradictory in-flight commands. This specifies the exception to the nominal Cooldown-then-Off sequence above.

**Additional acceptance AC-021:** after requesting stop and waiting with the previous heating state, publish burner `Off` directly without an intermediate `Cooldown` event: leave pending, adopt observed state and reconcile without user action, an extra command or invented past phase/duration. Also verify very late recovery and guards before any subsequent ON. HVAC OFF alone without usable burner observation does not satisfy this scenario.

**Dry-contact acknowledgement approved on 2026-11-05:** after requesting stop, usable relay `OFF` observation confirms the command, ends confirmation pending and starts minimum-OFF and cooldown counters at that observation, not command dispatch. Cooling is only presumed; acknowledgement does not prove physical stove shutdown. Repeated unchanged OFF publications do not reset counters. Acknowledgement itself issues no command and does not waive restart guards or contradictory in-flight command constraints. Without observed OFF, remain pending indefinitely; actual unavailability follows Q-002.

**Additional acceptance AC-022:** request OFF from an ON dry contact, then delay state feedback: remain pending without dating cooling from dispatch. Observe OFF: acknowledge without another command and initialize minimum-OFF and cooldown counters to zero at this observation. Repeat OFF and verify counters progress without resets; do not claim physical stove shutdown or allow ON before current demand, minimum OFF plus cooldown and other guards. Cover very late acknowledgement and Q-002 unavailability.

**Start pending and acknowledgement approved on 2026-11-05:** after requesting ON, expose “awaiting start confirmation”, separately from last observed state. Dry contact: usable observed relay ON acknowledges the command, ends pending and starts minimum ON at that observation; it means active contact/heating requested only, not verified heating. Duepi-EVO: observing a burner startup phase acknowledges the request and ends pending without waiting for actual heating; continue tracking phases and their protections. Service return or requested HVAC `heat` alone is not confirmation. Do not date transition counters from dispatch alone, repeat ON on every evaluation, reset counters on stable observations or issue acknowledgement-only commands. Pending alone with an available underlying does not mean `unavailable`; actual unavailability follows Q-002. Explicit OFF/safety retains priority; contradictory in-flight commands remain a separate decision.

**Additional acceptance AC-023:** request ON then delay feedback: retain pending and last observed state without invented heating/transition timestamps or retries induced solely by delay. Dry contact: observed ON acknowledges, starts minimum ON and remains an unverified heat request. Duepi-EVO: an observed startup phase acknowledges without waiting for actual heating; retain phase-specific protections. Verify no acknowledgement-only order, stable counters on repeated observations, availability while pending and OFF/safety priorities. Exact Duepi-EVO phase mapping and cases where startup is not observed remain design/decision follow-up.

**Direct heating observation, approved on 2026-11-05:** after requesting ON, a usable Duepi-EVO burner heating-phase observation also confirms startup and triggers automatic reconciliation, even without observed startup phase. Leave pending and adopt the reported phase without requiring the missed phase, inventing its duration or backdating counters. Requested HVAC `heat` alone is insufficient. Acknowledgement issues no command; retain applicable minimum durations and OFF/safety priorities. This settles earlier open references to unobserved startup phases.

**Additional acceptance AC-024:** after requesting ON and waiting with previous stopped state, publish a usable burner heating phase directly without an intermediate startup event: acknowledge, leave pending and reconcile without user action, an extra command or invented past phase/duration. Cover late observation, stable heating publications without counter resets and OFF/safety priorities. HVAC `heat` alone does not satisfy this scenario.

**Explicitly reported command error, approved on 2026-11-05:** for both ON and OFF, preserve last usable observed state and make the error visible, distinct from delayed confirmation. Infer neither completed transition nor certainty of no hardware effect from the error; do not date new counters from the failed call. Keep monitoring and automatically reconcile if the underlying changes despite the error, using approved acknowledgement observations and state-following rules. Do not automatically retry the failed command, including through periodic evaluations. A later relevant observation can confirm the transition without manual acknowledgement; keep the error accessible in diagnostics without maintaining a hold solely because of that historical error. History retention duration/channel is design work. A call error alone with an available underlying does not mean `unavailable`; actual unavailability follows Q-002. Explicit OFF/safety retains priority; contradictory commands remain open. Design must define HA error visibility without imposing retries or automatic escalation here.

**Additional acceptance AC-025:** inject explicit ON then OFF errors with an available underlying: verify preserved observed state, visible error distinct from ordinary pending, no invented transition/age and no automatic resend on repeated evaluations. Then change the underlying to a usable acknowledgement: automatically reconcile without an extra command or manual acknowledgement, keep the error accessible and apply counters/guards from observation. Without subsequent change, do not claim success. Separately verify Q-002 on actual unavailability and preserved explicit OFF/safety priorities.

**Explicit OFF/safety during pending ON, approved on 2026-11-05:** OFF immediately becomes the priority request without waiting for ON confirmation. Safety takes priority; neither pending startup nor minimum ON may delay this stop request. Stop treating the former ON intent as the request to satisfy. A late ON/startup/heating observation remains physical information to reconcile, but does not restore the former ON request or cancel priority OFF. Do not claim confirmed stop before the appropriate acknowledgement observation. If the underlying is unavailable, preserve this priority without claiming the stop was transmitted or executed; apply Q-002. Cancellation of an already transmitted ON and effective hardware execution order are not guaranteed by this decision: design must establish a verifiable mechanism to enforce OFF priority despite in-flight commands. No new automatic retry policy is introduced.

**Additional acceptance AC-026:** request ON then inject explicit OFF or safety OFF before acknowledgement: verify immediate OFF priority without waiting for ON confirmation or minimum ON. Then inject a late ON/startup/heating observation: adopt it without restoring the ON request or lifting priority OFF, and without false stop acknowledgement. Cover available and unavailable underlying states; distinguish decided priority, transmitted command and observed stop. Technical validation must include an already in-flight ON and demonstrate the chosen effective ordering without assuming cancellation.

**ON during an ongoing stop, approved on 2026-11-05:** simply and silently ignore any new ON request that violates the rules, notably while stop confirmation is pending. Do not interrupt stop, transmit ON, raise an alert for this normal rejection, retain deferred ON intent or schedule replay. Neither stop acknowledgement nor guard expiry may execute that ignored ON. A later ON decision can only come from a fresh evaluation of current demand under all guards and safety priorities; it is not replay of the rejected request. Preserve observed state: in the described case, the thermostat is still ON until stop is confirmed; receiving and ignoring ON is not a transition. This clarifies the previous proposal: do not retain a request to apply after stop confirmation. Silence applies to normal ON rejection, not explicitly reported command errors, which remain visible under AC-025.

**Additional acceptance AC-027:** during unconfirmed stop, inject one then multiple ON requests: verify no ON transmission, rejection alert, retained request, scheduled replay or state/counter change caused solely by rejection. Then confirm stop and let guards expire: without a new regulation decision, no ON is issued. Cover demand disappearing in the meantime and a fresh evaluation after stop: permit a new ON only if current demand and all rules allow it, never from a deferred request. Also verify no retention/replay of ON rejected by applicable guards; explicit errors remain separately covered by AC-025.

**Q-004 remains open for technical design:** in-flight ordering/cancellation mechanism and feasibility of controlling commands. Silent rejection without ON retention during stop, explicit OFF/safety priority during pending ON and functional command-error handling are decided; HA presentation and mechanisms remain design work. This decision adds no ON timeout or automatic retry. Stop acknowledgements and indefinite stop waiting remain decided. Implementation remains unauthorized.

## Q-003 decision of 2026-11-05: follow switch or climate underlying state

**Approved decision:** Pellet Stove naturally follows usable underlying state, whether a dry-contact `switch` or a `climate` such as Duepi-EVO. Adopt external actions observed during normal operation without a permanent hold or user acknowledgement required solely because of that action. Align internal state, zero the counters corresponding to the observed change, then continue regulation according to current demand, minimum durations and safety. Do not reset counters on repeated unchanged-state publications. Adoption alone must neither repeat the observed command nor issue an opposite command to restore the previous internal state.

**Explicit extension of Q-002:** counter persistence/recovery, suspension during unavailability with Pellet Stove `unavailable`, indefinite waiting and automatic reconciliation apply to both underlying types. Matching saved/retained state: resume counters; different state or unusable snapshot: adopt usable state and start the corresponding minimum durations. Relay ON/OFF wording in Q-002 below describes dry contact, not a requirement to reduce `climate` to a binary relay.

**Distinct semantics:** a `switch` exposes contact state only; external ON starts minimum ON and external OFF starts minimum OFF plus cooldown at observation. For `climate`, retain available burner/phase observations (ignition, heating, cooling, stopped) and their guards; do not equate requested HVAC mode with confirmed heating or HVAC OFF with completed cooling. Align internal state with these observations without replacing burner feedback with dry-contact presumptions or restarting the entire cycle at every phase change. Design must detail and test the observation-to-counter mapping.

**Diagnostics and limits:** expose the observed external change and reconciliation without claiming to identify its author or establish physical proof beyond supplied information. Missing, unknown or contradictory state does not become usable through adoption. Explicit OFF and safety retain priority; contradictory in-flight commands remain subject to Q-004. Underlying observations take precedence over a decision or service call, which does not confirm a transition.

**Additional acceptance AC-017:** with an available `switch`, inject external ON and OFF during normal operation: adopt each change, zero corresponding counters, respect minimum durations and safety, and continue without acknowledgement or adoption-only commands. Repeat stable observations and verify counters progress without resets or repeated commands. Intermediate unavailability follows Q-002.

**Additional acceptance AC-018:** repeat external following, restart/restoration and unavailability/recovery with a Duepi-EVO-like `climate` exposing burner/phase observations; verify adoption without reconciliation commands, preserved ignition/cooling guards, recovery of matching counters and initialization of relevant counters on changes. HVAC mode changes alone must not confirm heating or completed cooling; repeated stable-phase publications must not reset counters. Unclassifiable cases remain explicitly uncertain without invented ON permission.

**Q-003: functional decisions closed.** This decision overrides contrary open-Q-003 references, permanent hold/alert proposals after external actions and strictly unchanged `climate` behavior requirements in v0.3 below. Shared following/reconciliation is an intentional change; preserve specific commands, IDs, configuration and `climate` phase information. Q-004 and Q-005, technical feasibility and implementation authorization remain open. FR/EN guides describe this target as not implemented.

## Q-002 decision of 2026-11-05: continuity across restart

This user decision overrides version 0.3 wording below concerning restart recovery, especially FR-005/007/008/011, BR-001/002/003/004, AC-005/010 and §9. Other questions remain open; this decision does not authorize implementation.

- At HA shutdown, save cycle state and regulation counters: minimum ON, minimum OFF and presumed cooling. Save running counters, not just configured durations.
- Counters are paused while HA is stopped: downtime is not credited. At startup, resume exactly the saved values; this also applies to OFF and cooldown counters.
- If the relay is `unknown`/`unavailable` at startup or becomes unavailable at runtime, pause regulation decisions and counters **indefinitely**, until usable ON/OFF returns. During this wait, Pellet Stove itself shall be exposed as `unavailable`, not OFF; diagnostics shall make the cause accessible. Do not issue a default shutdown. Relay recovery, even much later, automatically triggers reconciliation before resuming: restore the startup snapshot or state retained before unavailability, then align it with the relay. If consistent, resume retained counters; otherwise do not blindly reuse previous-state counters or invent a transition time. The precise divergence policy remains undecided. Do not credit downtime. Neither the previously considered 5-minute timeout nor a 5-minute alert threshold is retained; no configurable delay is added. Explicit OFF/safety priorities remain applicable where commanding is possible, without guaranteeing delivery or making the relay available.
- At startup, restore saved values and resume the cycle where it stopped when the snapshot is usable and consistent with relay state. Restart alone must not restart a full ON, OFF or cooldown duration; this is regulation continuity, not evidence of physical state during downtime.
- An already-ON relay remains on for regulation. Restore its known ON counter without inventing a physical ignition time. Likewise, resume a consistent saved OFF/cooling counter instead of systematically requiring a new full OFF period.
- Neither HA shutdown nor Pellet Stove initialization/restoration alone shall trigger OFF or another ON. Explicit OFF, safety and justified regulation decisions after restoration still apply according to their priorities.
- Diagnostics distinguish configured durations, restored counters and recovery provenance. Saved logical age is not verified physical age.

**Additional acceptance AC-012:** save and restart during minimum ON, minimum OFF and cooldown; verify counter recovery without resetting counters or restarting a full duration. Example: 30-minute minimum ON, 20 elapsed minutes saved; 10 minutes remain, not 30. Verify no command caused solely by shutdown/restart, particularly no OFF for an ON relay; after restoration, only current demand, guards and priorities can justify a transition. Repeat a restart to verify counters do not start over.

**User report, not reproduced:** Pellet Stove tends to turn the stove off on restart. Implementation must identify the source of this OFF (plugin, lifecycle or VTherm), not assume restoration alone fixes it.

**Additional acceptance AC-013:** at startup and during runtime unavailability, Pellet Stove is `unavailable`, with no regulation ON/OFF decision or counter advancement while waiting. After more than 5 minutes and after a long outage, verify waiting and relay monitoring continue without automatic OFF. When usable ON/OFF returns, automatically reconcile before deciding, with no reconciliation-only command; resume retained counters if consistent, otherwise report divergence. Leave `unavailable` only after usable state and reconciliation; unresolved divergence shall not authorize ON. Do not add unavailable time to counters. No timeout or additional user action is required to detect late recovery.

**Hypothesis to investigate:** missing reconciliation might contribute to the reported restart shutdown; no cause has been established.

**Retained ON to observed OFF reconciliation, approved on 2026-11-05:** if saved or retained state was ON and the relay is OFF during reconciliation, adopt OFF. Start OFF and presumed-cooling counters at this usable observation, without crediting prior time or resuming an old OFF counter. The actual transition time remains unknown. Reconciliation itself issues no command. A new ON still requires current demand, minimum OFF plus cooldown and other guards, including unresolved contradictory in-flight commands (Q-004). This decision specifies the ON-to-OFF divergence mentioned above; the opposite case remains open.

**Additional acceptance AC-014:** restore or retain ON, then observe OFF when the relay returns; verify adoption of OFF without a reconciliation command, OFF and cooldown counters starting at zero at that observation, no invented OFF age and no ON before minimum OFF plus cooldown and clearance of other guards. Cover HA restart and recovery after runtime unavailability.

**General reconciliation rule, approved on 2026-11-05:** usable relay ON/OFF takes precedence over saved or retained state for regulation, without measuring flame or actual heating. If states agree, resume retained counters. If they differ, adopt observed state and start the corresponding minimum durations at this observation: OFF to ON, zero ON counter and a full minimum ON before ordinary OFF; ON to OFF, zero OFF and cooling counters and a full minimum OFF plus cooldown before another ON. Do not invent the actual transition time, credit unavailable time or issue a command solely because of reconciliation. Explicit OFF and safety retain priority; contradictory in-flight commands remain subject to Q-004. This settles open-divergence wording in the amendment above and overrides contrary v0.3 proposals below. Include this rule in FR/EN guides, marked as planned until implemented.

**Additional acceptance AC-015:** restore or retain OFF, then observe ON during reconciliation; verify adoption without a command, an ON counter starting at zero at that observation, a full minimum ON before ordinary OFF and explicit OFF/safety priority. Cover startup and recovery after unavailability without claiming to date physical ignition. When states agree, verify instead that retained counters are not reset.

**No usable snapshot, approved on 2026-11-05:** at first installation or after snapshot loss/invalidation, including after an abrupt stop, adopt the first usable relay ON/OFF and initialize its corresponding counters to zero at that observation. ON: full minimum ON before ordinary OFF; OFF: full minimum OFF plus cooldown before another ON, subject to current demand and other guards. Do not invent elapsed time or issue a command solely because of adoption. While the relay is unavailable, wait indefinitely with Pellet Stove `unavailable`. Explicit OFF and safety retain priority; contradictory in-flight commands remain subject to Q-004. An abrupt stop does not require resetting counters if a usable matching snapshot survives: resume its counters without reconstructing unsaved time.

**Additional acceptance AC-016:** without a usable snapshot (first installation, missing or invalid saved state after an abrupt stop), verify both ON and OFF: adoption without a command, corresponding counters starting at zero, full minimum durations and applicable priorities. Also verify indefinite `unavailable` waiting before the first usable state and, after an abrupt stop with a surviving usable matching snapshot, resumption of saved values without crediting unsaved time.

**Q-002: functional decisions closed on 2026-11-05.** This amendment overrides references to open Q-002 questions in v0.3 below. Usable snapshot and matching state: resume counters; different state or unusable snapshot: adopt relay state and zero its corresponding counters; unavailability: wait indefinitely with paused counters and Pellet Stove `unavailable`, then automatically reconcile. Q-003 through Q-005, command feasibility and implementation authorization remain open.

## 1. Context and objectives

Two users report that a VTherm `over_switch` controlling a `switch` relay works with standard TPI, but stays at `on_percent: 0` / `physical_state_not_confirmed_off` with `pellet_regulation`. Today the plugin admits only certain underlying `climate` states as observations. The objective is an explicitly selected **dry-contact** mode for a VTherm `over_switch` with one bistable `switch`: request ON/OFF without an intermediate generic thermostat, observing the contact and applying existing durations. A new ON means active contact and heating requested, without presuming the stove is on even after waiting; when the relay is already ON at HA startup, the stove is considered “on for regulation” immediately, with no transition phase or invented start time. This convention proves neither flame nor actual heating. OFF starts presumed cooling, whose completion is presumed only after `cooldown_duration_min` from a reliably observed OFF. Neither flame nor actual shutdown is measured. The existing `climate` mode remains unchanged. Resumption and conflict policies in §9 require decisions before implementation.

| FR-012 | The system shall preserve existing `climate` entries' behavior and IDs, without automatic activation, silent conversion, command changes or lost settings on migration; voluntary switching to dry contact shall not silently carry over power-level control from an old entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| FR-013 | Before delivery the FR/EN guides shall explain both paths (climate / dry contact), the warning, default switch ON/OFF commands, the role of existing cooldown and minimum times under user responsibility, lack of physical feedback and uncertainty/resumption conditions; no new duration shall be documented. Guides are not changed in this documentation phase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

## 4. Business rules

| ID     | Condition, exception and priority                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BR-001 | `switch.on/off` describes only the bistable relay's exposed state: a newly observed ON = **active contact / heating requested**, never proof of an ignited stove even after waiting; at HA startup, if the relay is already ON, the stove is immediately **considered on for regulation**, without transition phase or inferred start time, never as physical proof. Reliably and continuously observed OFF = **presumed cooling in progress**, then **presumed cooling complete** after `cooldown_duration_min` from that observation, never proof that the stove is off. `unknown`, `unavailable`, contradictory state or restored/unreliable timestamp proves neither observation continuity nor physical state. |
| BR-002 | An ordinary ON requires a current heating demand, an available relay, no conflicting ON command still in flight, and a reliable continuously observed OFF period lasting `min_off_duration_min + cooldown_duration_min`. Missing or merely restored OFF history is not elapsed time. At startup without reliable OFF history, only a new complete observed OFF period can lift this block, subject to Q-002/004.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| BR-003 | An ordinary OFF requested by regulation shall respect `min_on_duration_min`, the **minimum contact activation time** for short-cycle protection since a reliably observed ON, not ignition time. A relay already ON at HA startup is considered on for regulation without a known activation age: how the ON minimum applies to this ordinary OFF remains to be decided (Q-002), without inventing a start time. Explicit HVAC OFF and thermal safety override this minimum and BR-002; an elapsed deadline never requests a command on its own.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| BR-004 | After any OFF request, presumed cooling and the cooldown and restart deadlines start no earlier than reliable observation of relay OFF; a request or completed call without known outcome does not bring them forward. Unavailability or command failure: no new automatic ON; a safety OFF may be requested if commanding is possible, without a guarantee of hardware shutdown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| BR-005 | Dry-contact mode is opt-in per VTherm entry; without an explicit choice `climate` behavior is unchanged. A VTherm-specific entry takes complete precedence over global values; it shall not silently receive dry-contact mode from global settings. Presentation of the mode on the global entry remains to be decided (Q-005).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## 5. Functional constraints

- Safety: the plugin has neither flame detection nor cooling-completion or shutdown confirmation. The user shall set `cooldown_duration_min` and existing minimum times according to the stove manual and monitor external actions; no configured duration alone guarantees physical state. OFF priority does not guarantee hardware delivery.
- Compatibility: only one bistable `switch` underlying entity is in scope; existing `climate` entries and behavior are preserved. Neither pulse commands nor assumed hardware inversion are supported.
- Availability / experience: the warning shall be readable before confirmation in FR/EN; diagnostics shall remain useful during uncertainty. Maximum downtime and allowable numeric bounds: unknown.
- Privacy / regulation: no new personal information or regulatory constraint established; alerts and diagnostics shall not expose additional sensitive information.

Reference EN warning before confirmation: “The dry contact does not report the stove's actual state: ON means a heating request, not confirmed ignition. If the relay is already ON at startup, the stove is considered on for regulation only, without proof of actual heating or a known start time. After OFF, cooling completion is only presumed once the cooldown has elapsed. Set this cooldown and the minimum on/off times for your stove: too short a cooldown may cause reignition during cooling.”

## 7. Out-of-scope features

- Reliable feedback on flame, end of ignition or cooling: unavailable on the target stove and expressly excluded by user decision; no physical evidence will be invented. No new ignition/heating or shutdown delay is in scope: only existing minimum times and cooldown apply.
- Multiple relays, `input_boolean`, pulse commands, power levels and power boost on a `switch`: different contract or inapplicable to a simple dry contact.
- Changes to VTherm, Duepi, firmware or stove, and synchronizing a setpoint with a generic thermostat: not needed for the chosen chain.
- A minimal fix treating `switch.off` as immediate physical shutdown: rejected in the review because it skips presumed cooling.

## 8. Proposed future evolution

- Consider multiple contacts or actual physical feedback if another appliance provides it, after defining a distinct contract and safety rules; outside issue #9.
- Consider help setting existing minimum times and cooldown or enhanced monitoring alerts after field experience; no universal duration is established. No new timing setting is proposed at this stage.

## 9. Assumptions, open questions and traceability

**Decisions approved on 2026-11-03:** D-001, only relay and existing durations form the model, with no physical feedback: a new ON is active contact without an inference that the stove is on; OFF starts presumed cooling until cooldown expires, after which only its completion is presumed; D-002, dry-contact opt-in in the menu and visible FR/EN warning before confirmation; D-003, the user sets existing minimum times and cooldown and documentation explains limitations; Q-001, no new ignition/heating or shutdown delay, `min_on_duration_min` stays a short-cycle minimum and restart waits `min_off_duration_min + cooldown_duration_min` from reliable OFF; Q-002, a relay already ON at HA startup immediately counts as “on for regulation”, with no transition or assigned start time and without physical proof. Review approved for specification and design, not implementation.

**Specification assumptions to confirm:** H-001, a new ordinary ON can resume only after a new complete observed OFF period when history is doubtful; H-002, despite immediate adoption of “on for regulation” at startup with relay already ON, handling ordinary OFF remains undecided without inventing how long the contact has been active; H-003, opt-in applies to a VTherm-specific entry and not automatically to the global entry. These are proposed conservative rules, not user-approved decisions; the Q-002 startup convention itself is approved.

**Blocking questions for an approvable design:**

- Q-001 (decided on 2026-11-03): no new duration setting; keep existing units, bounds and values of `min_on_duration_min`, `min_off_duration_min` and `cooldown_duration_min`. A new ON never becomes “presumed on” after waiting; the Q-002 exception concerns only a relay already ON at HA startup and only regulation. Reliable OFF starts presumed cooling cooldown, whose completion remains a presumption; restart requires minimum OFF plus cooldown. Reported 1800 s TPI settings prove no physical stove timing. Choosing durations suitable for the stove remains the user's responsibility.
- Q-002 (partly decided on 2026-11-03): if the relay is already ON at HA startup, the stove is immediately considered “on for regulation”, without a transition phase, an invented activation time or flame proof. Open: how `min_on_duration_min` applies to ordinary OFF when the age of initial ON is unknown, and whether the same convention applies after relay unavailability. No continuity can be inferred from restored timestamps or an unavailable interval; a fresh first OFF observation starts a new complete OFF period without backdating.
- Q-003: how should external ON conflicting with logical state be handled: block/alert, adopt relay state, or request OFF? What exact rule returns to regulation after an external action? Recommendation: alert and block automatic ignition while conflict persists.
- Q-004: if safety OFF fails, relay is unavailable, or an ON is in flight, what visible escalation and observable ordering should be required, without promising hardware shutdown? Should an observable command and cancellation/ordering contract be obtained from VTherm/HA, or should the safety requirement be explicitly revised if that contract is unavailable? An observed OFF does not rule out a delayed ON; logical OFF priority is not an execution guarantee. Feasibility and testability of this race remain to be established before approval.
- Q-005: should the mode also be offered on the global entry, and how should activation of an old entry with power options present/neutralize those options? How can uniqueness and bistable type be checked without pretending to detect hardware? Recommendation: explicit mode per VTherm, power/boost neutralized, out-of-contract underlyings rejected.
